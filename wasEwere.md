Frase no passado

I was a teacher. -> Eu ERA/FUI um professor. -> Was I a teacher?
You were my friend. -> Você ERA/FOI meu amigo. ->  Were You my friend?
He was in a happy day. -> Ele ESTAVA em um dia feliz. -> Was He in a happy day? 
She was very cool.-> Ela ERA muito feliz. -> Was She very cool?
It WAS a sunny day. -> FOI/ERA um dia ensolarado. -> Was It a sunny day?
We WERE all friends here. -> Nós ÉRAMOS/FOMOS todos amigos aqui. -> Were We all friends here?
They WERE a tall and proud people. -> Eles ERAM/FORAM um povo alto e orgulhoso. -> Were They a tall and proud people?

You were not my friend. -> You weren't my friend.

It was not a sunny day. / It wasn't a sunny day.
